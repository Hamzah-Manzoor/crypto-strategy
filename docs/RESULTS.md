## Notes

### Phase 5: first in-sample results, v1 settings (2026-10-06)

**Context:** in-sample (Oct 2023 → Oct 2025) was a huge bull market. The 5 coins rose **+433%** on average. Buy & hold made **+429%**, but its wallet fell **51%** from its peak along the way.

**What we saw (1h, 0.1% fee):**
- **Only `MacdVolumeV1` made money:** +18%, max drawdown only 7.6%, Calmar 1.15.
  - It's in the market only 8% of the time, which is why it's nowhere near buy & hold's profit.
  - Its Calmar (1.15) is still below buy & hold's (2.53). In a bull run that strong, sitting in cash is expensive.
- **The other 6 lost money,** from −4% (EMA Trend) to −42% (RSI + Bollinger).
- **4h is better and 15m is much worse.** Same strategies, fewer and longer trades on 4h. On 15m, Trend Pullback made 7,440 trades and lost 89%.
- **No "too good to be true" flags.** No result is suspicious; if anything they're too bad.

**The diagnosis: it's mostly fees.** We re-ran with zero fees (marked *diagnostic* in the log; not a valid result):

| Strategy (1h) | Trades | Profit @ 0.1% fee | Profit @ 0 fee | Fees cost | Profit factor @ 0 fee |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TrendPullbackV1 | 1941 | −22.7% | **+66.1%** | 88.8 pp | 1.30 |
| MacdVolumeV1 | 695 | +18.1% | **+53.8%** | 35.7 pp | 1.41 |
| EmaTrendV1 | 675 | −4.2% | +26.2% | 30.4 pp | 1.14 |
| DonchianBreakoutV1 | 1329 | −27.3% | +19.4% | 46.7 pp | 1.05 |
| SqueezeBreakoutV1 | 403 | −13.9% | +1.5% | 15.4 pp | 1.02 |
| AdxSupertrendV1 | 240 | −13.9% | −6.0% | 8.0 pp | 0.92 |
| RsiBbReversionV1 | 699 | −41.9% | −23.3% | 18.6 pp | 0.87 |

**What this means, in plain words:**
- Most of these ideas **do have a small edge**, but it's about **0.1–0.3% per trade**, roughly the same as the **0.2% round-trip fee**. Thousands of trades hand that edge to the exchange.
- Two ideas (**ADX Supertrend** and **RSI + Bollinger**) lose even with free trading, at 1h and 15m. Their signals don't work in this market.
- **The way forward is fewer, better trades:** stricter entry filters, longer holding periods, higher timeframes. Shaving thresholds won't fix it. Phase 6 tuning optimises *after* fees, so it should push in that direction by itself.

**Checks behind these numbers:**
- 80 spot-checked trades entered exactly at the next candle's open price.
- The wallet-based drawdown matches Freqtrade's own wallet statistics.
- Fees were read from every trade, not from the config copy, which turned out to be wrong for `--fee` runs (fixed).
