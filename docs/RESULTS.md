## Notes

### Phase 5: first in-sample results, v1 settings (2026-10-06)

**Context:** in-sample (Oct 2023 → Oct 2025) was a huge bull market. The 5 coins rose **+433%** on average. Buy & hold made **+429%**, but its wallet fell **51%** from its peak along the way.

**What we saw (1h, 0.1% fee):**
- **Only `MacdVolumeV1` made money:** +18%, max drawdown only 7.6%, Calmar 1.15.
  - It's in the market only 8% of the time, which is why it's nowhere near buy & hold's profit.
  - Its Calmar (1.15) is still below buy & hold's (2.53). In a bull run that strong, sitting in cash is expensive.
- **The other 6 lost money,** from −4% (EMA Trend) to −42% (RSI + Bollinger).
- **4h is better and 15m is much worse.** Same strategies, fewer and longer trades on 4h. On 15m, Trend Pullback made 7,440 trades and lost 89%.
- **No "too good to be true" flags.** No result is suspicious; if anything they're too bad.

**The diagnosis: it's mostly fees.** We re-ran with zero fees (marked *diagnostic* in the log; not a valid result):

| Strategy (1h) | Trades | Profit @ 0.1% fee | Profit @ 0 fee | Fees cost | Profit factor @ 0 fee |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TrendPullbackV1 | 1941 | −22.7% | **+66.1%** | 88.8 pp | 1.30 |
| MacdVolumeV1 | 695 | +18.1% | **+53.8%** | 35.7 pp | 1.41 |
| EmaTrendV1 | 675 | −4.2% | +26.2% | 30.4 pp | 1.14 |
| DonchianBreakoutV1 | 1329 | −27.3% | +19.4% | 46.7 pp | 1.05 |
| SqueezeBreakoutV1 | 403 | −13.9% | +1.5% | 15.4 pp | 1.02 |
| AdxSupertrendV1 | 240 | −13.9% | −6.0% | 8.0 pp | 0.92 |
| RsiBbReversionV1 | 699 | −41.9% | −23.3% | 18.6 pp | 0.87 |

**What this means, in plain words:**
- Most of these ideas **do have a small edge**, but it's about **0.1–0.3% per trade**, roughly the same as the **0.2% round-trip fee**. Thousands of trades hand that edge to the exchange.
- Two ideas (**ADX Supertrend** and **RSI + Bollinger**) lose even with free trading, at 1h and 15m. Their signals don't work in this market.
- **The way forward is fewer, better trades:** stricter entry filters, longer holding periods, higher timeframes. Shaving thresholds won't fix it. Phase 6 tuning optimises *after* fees, so it should push in that direction by itself.

**Checks behind these numbers:**
- 80 spot-checked trades entered exactly at the next candle's open price.
- The wallet-based drawdown matches Freqtrade's own wallet statistics.
- Fees were read from every trade, not from the config copy, which turned out to be wrong for `--fee` runs (fixed).

### Phase 6: validation (2026-10-06)

**What was tried, all on in-sample data only:**
- **11 tuned versions** (`…T`). Hyperopt with Calmar loss, 300 epochs, seed 42, 0.1% fee.
- **4 hand-designed v2s** aimed at fewer trades: decisions on 4h candles, 4h uptrend filter, longer holds.
- **10 candidates picked by a fixed in-sample rule**, written down *before* the out-of-sample look: profit > 0, profit factor ≥ 1.1, at least 50 trades.

**Stage 1: one look at out-of-sample** (Oct 2025 → Oct 2026). Buy & hold lost **35%** there, falling **63%** at its worst.

| Strategy | In-sample | Out-of-sample | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `EmaTrendV2` (hand-designed, untuned) | +21.5% | **+5.1%** (max DD 11.9%) | **PASS** |
| `SqueezeBreakoutV1T` | +2.6% | +1.2% (max DD 2.4%) | REVIEW (tiny drawdown, ~1% time in market) |
| `AdxSupertrendV1T` | +14.4% | +1.6% | FAIL (profit factor 1.09, edge faded) |
| `EmaTrendV2T` (tuned) | **+72.1%** | −1.0% | FAIL |
| `DonchianBreakoutV2T` (tuned) | **+70.4%** | −8.2% | FAIL |
| `MacdVolumeV1T` (tuned) | +25.5% | −11.0% | FAIL |
| 4 others | +7% to +52% | −0.7% to −7.6% | FAIL |

**Stage 2: consistency and market moods.** Verdict: **no strategy promoted**.

| Strategy | Quarters profitable (rule ≥ 60%) | 8 quarters together | Buy & hold, same quarters | Worst regime drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `EmaTrendV2` (fixed settings) | 3 of 8 (38%) | +11.2% | +41.9% | 13.2% |
| `SqueezeBreakoutV1T` (re-tuned parent) | 3 of 8 (38%) | −5.9% | +41.9% | 2.0% |
| *`EmaTrendV2` re-tuned every quarter (information only)* | *5 of 8 (62%)* | *+23.3%* | *+41.9%* | *efficiency 0.49, just under 0.5* |

**What we learned, in plain words:**
1. **Tuning overfits.** The three biggest in-sample winners (+72%, +70%, +26%) all **lost money** out-of-sample. The tuner found settings that matched 2023–25 noise. Several tuned stops sat at the edge of the allowed range (−3% or about −15%), which was a warning sign in hindsight.
2. **Structural changes generalised better than tuning.** The hand-designed `EmaTrendV2` (4h decisions, fewer trades) was the only clean Stage 1 pass, *with its default settings*.
3. **The survivors protect money in crashes but earn little.** In the out-of-sample bear market (Nov 2025 → Feb 2026, basket −45%), `EmaTrendV2` finished at −0.1%. Over the full out-of-sample year it made +5%, while buy & hold lost 35%. In bull markets it captures only a small part of the rise.
4. **Trend-following profits come in lumps.** `EmaTrendV2` made all its money in 3 strong quarters and lost a little in the other 5. Our consistency rule (60% of quarters profitable) was set in advance and wasn't relaxed after seeing this.
5. **The most promising lead** is `EmaTrendV2` re-tuned every quarter: 62% of quarters profitable and +23% over 2 years, but an efficiency of 0.49 against a 0.5 minimum. It's a candidate for a proper, pre-registered test later. It doesn't count now.

**Out-of-sample looks used:** 9 runs (Stage 1: 3; walk-forwards: 3; out-of-sample regime windows: 3). All are logged with 👁 in the experiment log.

### Robustness check, calibration (2026-10-07)

- **New gate:** `lab.py robustness <Strategy>`, on dev data. A strategy must survive 4 kinds of poking:
  - **R1:** a different rebalance time, or acting late.
  - **R2:** nearby settings.
  - **R3:** leaving one coin out.
  - **R4:** removing its 3 best trades.
- **All three calibration strategies came out ROBUST.** Plan, results and the 2 tooling bugs fixed along the way: `docs/research/robustness-check-plan.md`.
- **Correction to Round 3:** momentum rotation is **not** timing-fragile. It's profitable at all 28 rebalance times (+207% … +520% on dev). The earlier claim compared just two times, one of them the second-worst.
- **Momentum rotation is still not promoted:** its drawdowns (~40%) break the 30% safety rule.
